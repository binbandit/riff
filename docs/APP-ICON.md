# App icon

The iPad icon lives in `Riff/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`. It is an opaque 1024 × 1024 PNG. Its background fills the square; iPadOS applies the home-screen mask.

Created with the built-in image generation tool, then resized to the asset catalog's required dimensions. A custom ivory lowercase “r” sits on a vermilion field, with a flowing shoulder that suggests a sound wave.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a final production icon for Riff, an independent playful iPad soundboard app. One square opaque 1024x1024 image, background fills all corners, the operating system will apply the corner mask. Sophisticated minimal graphic design, striking at tiny sizes. Solid vivid vermilion red background #E84432. Center one oversized warm ivory custom lowercase r monogram, designed as a single thick continuous fluid sound-wave stroke: substantial vertical stem with a beautifully rounded bottom, smoothly curved arched shoulder extending to the right and a short downward-rounded terminal, suggesting a little audio cable or wave. The r must read immediately as an r, with confident geometric proportions and a lively slightly forward-leaning stance. Broad smooth silhouette, optical balance, generous negative space, mark about 55 percent of canvas width and 60 percent of height. Flat vector-like finish, perfectly clean edges, just two solid colors. This is a beautifully drawn custom letterform, not a typed font. Aim for the distilled memorable quality of an independent music instrument brand. Only this one ivory symbol and red field. No soundboard grid, no tiles, no 3D, no shadows, no gradients, no gloss, no texture, no extra text, no rounded-square frame, no border, no mockup.

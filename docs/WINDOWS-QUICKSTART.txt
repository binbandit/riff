RIFF - YOUR IPAD, YOUR CONTROL ROOM

1. Extract the whole ZIP to a folder on your Windows 11 PC.
2. Open Riff.Companion.exe. No separate .NET installation is needed.
3. Allow Riff on Private networks if Windows Firewall asks.
4. Open Riff on your iPad. Tap Connect your PC, scan the QR code, then Connect PC.
   Both devices must be on the same home network. Choose your Wi-Fi/Ethernet IP
   in the companion, rather than a VPN adapter.
5. Tap a sound. Use Edit deck on the iPad to add, customize, or move buttons.

TO PLAY SOUNDS AS YOUR VOICE IN GAME CHAT

Install VB-CABLE from https://vb-audio.com/Cable/ and restart Windows.
In Riff Audio & voice chat, choose CABLE Input and Apply.
In your game or Discord, choose CABLE Output as the microphone.
Use voice activation, or hold your push-to-talk key while a sound plays.
Use the chat app's microphone test to verify the complete route.
If sounds get cut off, lower the voice threshold and disable noise suppression.

To hear the sounds yourself, enable Listen to this device for CABLE Output in
Windows Sound > Recording > Properties > Listen; choose headphones for playback.
This route uses clips instead of your real microphone. Mixing both requires an
optional mixer such as Voicemeeter. VB-CABLE and Voicemeeter are separate products.

DECKS FOR DIFFERENT GAMES

Create or duplicate a deck on iPad, then link a Steam game in Deck settings.
Enable Follow my Steam game in Settings. Steam must be open. Detection is best
effort using Steam's local data; if multiple games run, select a deck manually.
Manual selection stays in place until another game is detected.

Closing the companion window leaves Riff running in the tray. Use Quit Riff in
its tray menu to stop it. Stop all cancels sounds and remaining sequence steps.
Allowed apps lets you select the .exe applications your iPad may launch.

Keep your pairing link private. Revoke paired devices generates a new key.
Do not forward port 49321 on your router. Riff is intended for your local network.
Your decks and clips are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Riff. Close Riff before backing up.

This is an unsigned personal build. Sign and install Riff-iPad-unsigned.ipa with
your own Apple account using Sideloadly on Windows (https://sideloadly.io/), or
build with Xcode on a Mac. Free signing requires renewal every 7 days. The
physical sideloading flow has not been tested here. See the README for details.
